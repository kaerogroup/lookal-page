# LOOKaL AI Engine Optimization (AIEO)

## Purpose

AIEO helps search and answer engines understand what LOOKaL is, what it currently does, what evidence supports those claims, and what is only part of the future roadmap.

AIEO does not replace SEO. It builds on crawlability, useful content, entity clarity, semantic HTML, structured data, evidence and trustworthy claims.

## Core answer LOOKaL should be understood as

LOOKaL is a community screen advertising network for local businesses. Advertisers can choose locations, choose campaign days and manage campaigns digitally, while ads appear on physical screens in real premises.

Short positioning:

`Mudah macam iklan online. Hadir di dunia sebenar.`

## Answer-engine principles

1. Answer important user questions directly in visible HTML.
2. Keep one clear entity name: LOOKaL.
3. Connect LOOKaL to its operator, Uniras Sdn Bhd.
4. Describe Snap2Ads as part of the LOOKaL campaign workflow, not as a separate unrelated product.
5. Use real operational evidence: screen photos, location photos and walkthrough videos.
6. Keep pricing, specifications and campaign mechanics consistent across visible copy and structured data.
7. Distinguish ad plays from audience impressions.
8. Distinguish current capability from future sensor/audience-intelligence vision.
9. Prefer original operational knowledge over generic industry filler.
10. Keep pages crawlable and canonical.

## Keyword model

Keywords are discovery language, not a density target.

Primary concepts:
- iklan skrin komuniti
- iklan skrin digital
- iklan perniagaan tempatan
- iklan di premis
- pilih lokasi iklan
- rangkaian skrin komuniti

Use synonyms and natural language around the same entities rather than repeating one exact phrase.

Do not:
- add a meta keywords tag
- hide keyword blocks
- create hundreds of thin query pages
- repeat city names without genuine location content
- write content primarily to manipulate search or AI responses

## Direct-answer architecture

Questions worth answering in visible content include:
- Apa itu LOOKaL?
- Bagaimana LOOKaL berfungsi?
- Berapa harga untuk mula?
- Boleh pilih satu lokasi sahaja?
- Berapa lama minimum kempen?
- Boleh upload poster sendiri?
- Boleh guna video?
- Bagaimana tayangan direkodkan?
- Apa beza LOOKaL dengan online ads?
- Bagaimana pemilik premis boleh menyertai rangkaian?

Answers should be short first, then expanded only when useful.

## Structured data

Current entity graph:
- Organization
- WebSite

Structured data must match visible copy. It is supporting machine-readable context, not a substitute for useful page content.

## AI crawler discovery

The public robots policy allows normal crawling and explicitly allows `OAI-SearchBot`.

Crawler access alone does not guarantee inclusion, ranking, citation or recommendation by an AI system.

## Future audience intelligence

Future sensor capabilities must be described as roadmap/vision until verified in production.

Potential aggregate signals:
- presence
- footfall patterns
- dwell time
- time-of-day patterns
- estimated viewing opportunities
- broad audience context

Do not claim:
- measured impressions without validated measurement
- demographic precision that has not been tested
- facial recognition
- individual identity tracking

The intended architecture is privacy-first and aggregate.

## Measurement

Track separately where available:
- organic search sessions
- branded search demand
- landing-page conversions
- ChatGPT referral traffic
- AI/search citations observed manually
- indexed pages
- queries and impressions from Search Console

Do not invent an "AI ranking score". AI answer visibility can vary by query, user context, model and time.

## Release gate

Before calling AIEO production-accepted:
1. canonical domain serves the intended landing page
2. robots.txt resolves publicly
3. sitemap.xml resolves publicly
4. important visible content is crawlable
5. structured data validates and matches visible claims
6. primary campaign facts are internally consistent
7. Search Console is configured
8. AI crawler access is not accidentally blocked by CDN or bot protection
